Post your own videos to TikTok from a link

TT automation is a small personal tool run by one creator. It takes a link to a video the creator owns, downloads the file, and posts it to the creator's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Step 1Paste a linkto the creator's own video
Step 2Downloadthe video file locally
Step 3Post to TikTokvia the Content Posting API

How TikTok is used

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): the creator signs in with TikTok and authorizes the tool. Only the open ID, display name and avatar are read, to show which account is connected.
  • Content Posting API (video.publish): videos are posted directly to the authorized creator's own profile, only when the creator starts an upload.

Who can use it

This tool is for the creator's own account only. It is not offered to the public and does not post on behalf of other users.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

What we collect

When you connect TikTok, the tool receives an access token and your basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) through Login Kit. It also handles the video files you choose to post.

How it is used

This data is used only to post videos you select to your own TikTok account and to show which account is connected. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored locally on the operator's own computer. Downloaded video files are kept locally only until they are posted, and can be deleted at any time.

Third parties

Videos are sent to TikTok through its official API. A video download service may be used to fetch the video from the link you provide.

Your choices

You can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's settings under Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. Revoking access stops all posting.
